Complete Banana Farming Guide

1. Land Preparation

Soil: Deep, well-drained fertile loam. pH 5.5 - 6.5. Avoid waterlogging.
Hole size: Dig 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Mix with manure.
Spacing: 3m x 3m. That’s 440 plants per acre.

2. Planting & Early Care

When: Plant anytime with irrigation. Best at start of rains.
How: Remove from bag carefully. Plant at same depth.
Watering: Water 2x per week. Mulch to retain moisture.
De-suckering: Keep only 3 stems: mother, daughter, granddaughter.

3. Fertilizer Application

At planting: 1 debe manure + 200g DAP per hole.
Every 3 months: 200g CAN per plant.
Organic: Apply manure 2x per year. Compost tea boosts growth.

4. Pest & Disease Management

Pests: Banana weevils, nematodes. Tissue culture is disease free.
Diseases: Sigatoka, Panama disease. Remove infected leaves.
Tip: Keep farm clean. Tissue culture gives 100% uniformity and early maturity.

5. Harvesting

When: Matures in 9-12 months after planting.
How: Cut bunch when fingers are full. Support with pole.
Yield: 20-40kg per bunch. 1 acre can give 300+ bunches.

6. Marketing

Local market: Sell at KSh 800-1500 per bunch at farm gate.
Value add: Chips, flour, juice. High demand in towns.
