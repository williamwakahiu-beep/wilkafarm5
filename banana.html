Banana Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Mix with 1 debe manure. Space 3m x 3m apart. Bananas need fertile, well-drained soil and lots of water.

2. Planting & Watering

Plant tissue culture plantlets in afternoon. Water 2-3x per week. Mulch to retain moisture. Desucker to leave 1 mother, 1 follower, 1 grandchild.

3. Pest & Disease Control

Common pests: Nematodes, weevils. Use clean planting material.
Diseases: Sigatoka, Panama wilt. Remove infected leaves. Ensure proper drainage.
