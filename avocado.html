Avocado Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Mix with 1 debe manure + topsoil. Space 9m x 9m apart. Avocados need deep, well-drained red soil. Avoid waterlogging.

2. Planting & Watering

Plant at onset of rains. Water 2x per week for first 6 months. Mulch to retain moisture. Prune to maintain 1 main stem. Graft union should be 20cm above ground.

3. Pest & Disease Control

Common pests: Thrips, scales, fruit flies. Use neem spray.
Diseases: Root rot, anthracnose. Ensure drainage. Spray copper during flowering.
