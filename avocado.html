Complete Avocado Farming Guide

1. Land Preparation

Soil: Deep, well-drained red loam. pH 5.5 - 6.5. Avoid waterlogging.
Hole size: Dig 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Leave it open for 2 weeks to kill pests.
Mix: Fill with topsoil + 1 debe well-decomposed manure + 200g DAP.
Spacing: 9m x 9m for Hass/Fuerte. That’s 50 trees per acre.

2. Planting & Early Care

When: Plant at onset of long rains, March-April or Oct-Nov.
How: Remove polythene bag carefully. Plant with graft union 20cm above ground.
Watering: Water 2x per week for first 6 months. Then 1x per week.
Mulching: Use grass/dry leaves to keep moisture and suppress weeds.

3. Fertilizer Application

Year 1-2: 200g CAN every 3 months in a ring 30cm from stem.
Year 3+: 500g NPK 17:17:17 before rains. 300g Sulphate of Potash during flowering.
Organic: Apply 2 debe manure per tree yearly. Compost tea works well.

4. Canopy & Pruning Management

Year 1: Remove side branches up to 50cm to form 1 strong stem.
Year 2: Pinch top to encourage 3-4 main branches.
Mature trees: Prune after harvest to open center for sunlight and air. This reduces disease.

5. Pest & Disease Management

Pests: Thrips, scales, fruit flies, red spider mites. Spray neem or Thunder 2x per month.
Diseases: Root rot, anthracnose, powdery mildew. Ensure drainage. Spray copper-based fungicide during flowering.
Tip: Check leaves weekly. Send photos on WhatsApp 0710 279 318 for diagnosis.

6. Harvesting

When: Hass matures in 2-3 years. Harvest when skin turns dark purple.
How: Use secateurs. Leave 2cm stalk. Harvest early morning.
Yield: Mature tree gives 200-500 fruits per year.

7. Marketing

Local market: Sell to brokers at KSh 20-40 per fruit at farm gate.
Export: Grade by size. Exporters pay KSh 60-120 per fruit. Requires GlobalGAP.
Value add: Oil processing. We can link you to buyers. Call 0710 279 318
