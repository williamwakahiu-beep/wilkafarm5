Complete Macadamia Farming Guide

1. Land Preparation

Deep, well-drained soil, pH 5.0-6.5. Spacing 8m x 8m. 65 trees per acre.

2. Planting & Care

Plant at start of rains. Water weekly first 2 years. Mulch heavily.

3. Fertilizer

Apply NPK and manure yearly. Boron important for nut quality.

4. Pests & Diseases

Watch for stink bugs, husk spot. Spray as needed.

5. Harvesting

Matures in 3-4 years. Harvest fallen nuts. Yield: 20-40kg per tree.
