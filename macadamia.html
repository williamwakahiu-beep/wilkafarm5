Macadamia Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Mix with manure. Space trees 7m x 7m apart. Macadamia needs deep, well-drained, slightly acidic soil. Altitude 600-1600m.

2. Planting & Care

Plant at onset of rains. Provide windbreaks. Mulch heavily to conserve moisture. Apply fertilizer 4x per year. Prune to maintain 4-5m height.

3. Harvesting

Nuts fall naturally when mature. Collect every 2-3 days. Dry to 10% moisture content. Store in cool dry place. Harvesting season: Feb - June.
