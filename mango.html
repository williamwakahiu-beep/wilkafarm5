Mango Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 3ft x 3ft x 3ft. Mix soil with 2 debe of manure. Space trees 8m x 8m apart. Mangoes love deep, well-drained soils. Prepare 1 month before rains.

2. Planting

Plant at beginning of rains. Remove polythene without disturbing roots. Graft union 20cm above ground. Stake young trees against wind. Water thoroughly.

3. Pest & Disease Control

Common pests: Fruit flies, scale insects, mealy bugs.
Diseases: Anthracnose, powdery mildew. Prune for air circulation. Spray copper fungicide before flowering.
