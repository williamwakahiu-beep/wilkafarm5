Complete Mango Farming Guide

1. Land Preparation

Well-drained soil, pH 5.5-7.5. Spacing 10m x 10m. 40 trees per acre.

2. Planting & Care

Plant at start of rains. Water weekly. Prune to 3-4 main branches.

3. Fertilizer

Manure + NPK. Spray foliar during flowering.

4. Pests & Diseases

Watch for fruit flies, powdery mildew. Bag fruits.

5. Harvesting

Matures in 3-4 years. Harvest when mature. Yield: 500-1000 fruits per tree.
