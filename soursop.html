Soursop Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Space 4m x 4m apart. Likes warm, lowland areas with good rainfall. Avoid waterlogging.

2. Care & Maintenance

Prune to keep tree at 3-4m. Feed with manure + compost 3x per year. Protect young trees from strong winds.

3. Harvesting

Harvest when fruit turns from dark to light green. Handle gently. Used for juice, smoothies, and natural medicine.
