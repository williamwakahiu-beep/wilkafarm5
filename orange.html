Complete Orange Farming Guide

1. Land Preparation

Well-drained loam soil, pH 5.5-6.5. Spacing 6m x 6m. 111 trees per acre.

2. Planting & Care

Plant during rains. Water 2x per week. Mulch to retain moisture.

3. Fertilizer

Use NPK 17:17:17 and manure. Apply CAN during fruiting for bigger fruits.

4. Pests & Diseases

Watch for citrus canker, aphids, fruit flies. Spray regularly.

5. Harvesting

Matures in 2-3 years. Harvest when orange. Yield: 800-1000 fruits per tree.
