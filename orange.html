Orange Farming Tips

1. Land Preparation

Dig holes 2ft x 2ft x 2ft. Space 5m x 5m apart. Oranges need full sun and well-drained loam soil. pH 5.5 - 6.5.

2. Watering

Water deeply 1-2x per week. Mulch to retain moisture. Reduce water 2 months before harvest to sweeten fruits.

3. Pest & Disease Control

Common pests: Citrus aphids, leaf miners, fruit flies.
Diseases: Citrus canker, greening. Use certified seedlings. Spray neem oil for organic control.
